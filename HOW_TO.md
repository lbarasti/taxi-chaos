# How to play Taxi Chaos

Taxi Chaos runs in a browser or as the standalone `dist/index.html`. It works offline after the game has been built or loaded. Use a keyboard on desktop, or the on-screen controls on phones and tablets.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrow keys | Drive; on foot, move in screen directions |
| Space | Handbrake/drift in a car; rear brake on a motorbike |
| Shift + accelerate | Rechargeable nitro boost |
| E / mobile action | Exit or mount a nearby vehicle; pay at harbour security |
| A or D while pressing E | Choose which side to exit a moving vehicle |
| R | Optional tow and repair when near a wreck |
| H | Set or cancel a route to harbour security |
| G | Set or cancel a garage route; enter when stopped nearby |
| Tab / HUD clipboard below Texts | Open This shift (Tab again resumes) |
| P / Escape | Pause or resume |
| M | Mute or unmute sound effects |
| F2 | Open scenarios and the inspector |

On touch screens, drag the bottom-right joystick to steer, accelerate, or brake/reverse. Small sideways movements around the centre keep you driving straight; push further left or right to turn, with steering growing smoothly to full lock. It also moves you while on foot. Hold **BOOST** or **DRIFT** at the bottom left with your left thumb: BOOST is above DRIFT. Vehicle actions appear when available; tilt the joystick left or right before tapping **Jump** to choose a side when leaving a moving vehicle. The top bar and taxi menu have been removed. Pause and Test Lab currently require a keyboard; sound settings remain on the welcome/pause screens. There is no in-game fullscreen button.

## Your shift

Slow down inside a **yellow ring** to collect a passenger or parcel. Deliver to the **green ring** for base pay plus possible time and comfort tips. A late delivery can still be completed; the timer affects the bonus, not whether you get paid. Drifts and near-misses earn small skill bonuses, and nitro recharges automatically.

You start in Old Town near Pit Stop. Buy engine, grip, armor, and boost upgrades there, and repair damaged vehicles. Upgrades belong to the individual vehicle: repairs preserve them, another vehicle has its own levels, and explosion or sinking destroys them. Wallet and completed-job progress persist between runs, along with upgrades on surviving invested vehicles. **This shift**, opened with **Tab** or the HUD clipboard below Texts, shows earnings, completed jobs, smashed objects, and pedestrian fatalities since you last woke from sleep. The pause screen shows the same stats. These totals survive reloading and reset after sleeping.

### Open Shore, texts and your notebook

The campaign lasts **fourteen full days**, beginning at 07:00 and including the
fourteenth night. Time advances while driving. Park at Pit Stop and use
**This shift → Sleep at Pit Stop** to skip to the next 07:00. The menu previews known
bookings closing during sleep; passengers, pursuits and fires block sleeping.
Recovery, reload and run reset preserve the date.

Normal dispatch mixes ordinary jobs with recurring customers. **Texts** lets
you choose a booking with an exact pickup-before deadline; competing bookings
commit only when you stop at the deliberately selected pickup. This shift →
**Ask dispatch for an ordinary fare** skips a suggested story booking. Dialogue
continues during travel and at arrival. The HUD shows the calendar day alongside
its clock; overnight booking shifts still turn over at 07:00.

A persistent morning headline opens the latest unread newspaper. **Newsroom →
Publications** keeps the edition archive, including days when you did not pursue
an investigation. Reading pauses the game. Each edition keeps the findings and
public responses that were known when it printed; later events do not rewrite it.

The **Pause → Dispatch notebook** keeps signed receipts, ride conversations
and documents.
The café changes on the calendar whether or not you visit it. Search your own
records to revisit the details.

Route to the newsroom at **Old Town South**, stop nearby and leave selected
copies from the notebook. Source checks take **three game hours** and need
independent material. Return to ask Ruth to run a ready story in the next
**06:00 edition**. Publication and later public responses are separate; originals
remain in the notebook. The last edition is **Day 14 at 06:00**. The newsroom
shortcut opens its panel; routing is an explicit action inside it. Sources are
grouped into To submit, Being checked and Checked sources. General notes stay
in the notebook. Near an eligible observation site, activate the camera shortcut
to collect the scene deliberately; seeing it alone adds no record. The calendar
continues through fourteen nights, ending on Day 15 at 07:00.

At the end, a **Campaign complete** recap records both achieved and unresolved
outcomes. It waits for an active fare and immediate danger to pass before opening
automatically; a persistent notice also lets you open it yourself. Choose
**Continue in arcade mode** to keep your cash, garage and vehicle upgrades while
ordinary dispatcher work continues. The clock keeps running, and the recap and
edition archive remain available from the pause menu. Completion and unread
editions survive reloading.

## Explore the city

Use the radar and navigation markers to find jobs and destinations. Routes follow roads, and fare distance and timing account for the road network.

The **circular minimap** in the top-left corner is the whole navigation panel:
no header above it, no caption chip below it, no district banner or distance
counter drawn on the wheel. It shows a 320 m neighbourhood with your taxi (a
walker while on foot) at the centre, while the map's accessible label carries
the current neighbourhood, load and destination. Clock, cash, personal health
and police stars stay separate at the top right.

Destinations use one distinctive symbol each: a ringed circle for a pickup, a
diamond for a delivery, and drawn garage, newsroom, harbour and container
markers. A chevron on the rim points along the destination bearing, whether the
destination still sits on the wheel or beyond it. Once a passenger or parcel is
aboard, a soft green light stays within the cab roof's footprint, or lights a
small patch of ground underneath the courier bike without tinting its artwork. The
wheel itself stays free of cargo badges. While walking, parked
rides, the garage and the newsroom stay marked and no permanent instruction bar
covers the map.

The **shortcut menu** is separate from the map. The dispatcher button routes the
next fare or hides fare guidance, the speech bubble opens your texts, the clipboard below it opens This shift, and the
newspaper opens the newsroom panel, where you can choose a route. On a desktop
the shortcuts sit on the right edge; in a narrow desktop window (about 701–1100 px wide) they form two columns just to the
right of the map, clear of the right-hand conversation; on a portrait
phone they stack on the left above BOOST and DRIFT; on a landscape phone they
form a row across the top centre with the shift clipboard below Texts, shrinking the map so the controls stay clear. Shallow desktop windows use two columns below the personal status display.

Press the dispatcher button before pickup to hide fare guidance (and its route
and destination symbol) while you browse your texts and the newsroom; press it
again to route the fare. Hiding guidance does not cancel a booking or prevent a
physical pickup. Pressing it while the newsroom is routed clears that guidance
and returns to the fare.

- **Eastern bridges and highlands:** take the northern crossing to Pine Woods or the southern crossing to Eagle Highlands. Curved roads connect Pine Junction, Fern Hollow, Highland Lodge, and Eagle Lookout. F2 → **Eastern bridges / highlands** starts at Pine Bridge with a delivery to Eagle Lookout.
- **Woodland villas and Fairway Country Club:** explore the southern loop through Millionaires Row, Woodland Estates, and Cedar Woods, with private villa drives and a golf club. The golf pond sinks vehicles just like villa pools; walking routes keep clear of its shoreline. Hole flags can be knocked down by driving into their poles. F2 → **Woodland villas / golf club** starts in Woodland Estates with a passenger for Fairway Club.
- **Sunset Beach and Ocean Walk:** head to the southern waterfront for soft sand, breakable beach furniture, and a promenade between the beach and hotels. Road tyres lose traction in sand; the beach patrol pickup has off-road tyres. Cars and bikes can cross Ocean Walk and the sand around the hotels, just like city pavements; hotel walls and beach furniture remain obstacles. Surf and pools can drown the actor or sink a vehicle. F2 → **Sunset Beach / soft sand**, **Beach / off-road pickup**, or **Ocean Walk / hotel promenade**.
- **Northstar Harbour:** press **H** to navigate to security. In a healthy, non-burning car, stop out of police sight and pay **$75** with E or the mobile action. Follow cyan to **C-76**, drive fully inside and stay below 0.8 m/s for 2.5 seconds while concealed. The stock replacement clears police heat and retains your wallet and current fare; the exchanged car and its upgrades are no longer available. Another visit requires another payment. F2 → **Northstar Harbour / car exchange**.
- **Commercial traffic:** delivery vans, flatbeds, freight trucks, and fuel trucks share the roads as NPCs. Fuel cargo can ignite and cause a larger chain explosion. These vehicles cannot be taken by the player. F2 → **Vans / freight traffic** or **Flammable cargo / impact**.

A hard motorbike crash can separate the rider and bike. A healthy fallen bike
must be lifted before mounting; lifting does not repair it. A badly damaged or
burning bike cannot start. Use the nearby tow/repair action or find another ride.
Moving exits preserve momentum and may injure the rider on landing; at rest,
dismounting is immediate.

## Settings and sound

Pulp gore and difficulty can be changed in **Settings** from the welcome or pause screen. Sound effects start after your first click, tap, or key press. Adjust FX volume, mute, **Player engine**, and separate siren volume in the same Settings menu. Player engine changes only your vehicle's engine; 0% silences it while keeping tyres, boost, traffic and impacts audible. Audio preferences are saved independently from progress. Engines audibly shift through five gears; motorcycles and cars with engine upgrade level 2 or 3 have a sixth gear. There is no music or voice audio.

The world clock begins at **07:00** and advances while you play; pause, garage, and game-over states stop it. Lighting transitions through morning, afternoon, evening, and night. The time of day does not change fare or fire timers.

## Test Lab

Press **F2** to try scenarios, inspect debug views, adjust traffic, use cheats, or audition sound effects. Difficulty, gore, and audio preferences are in the welcome/pause **Settings** menu. Fixtures cover deliveries, vehicles, collisions, recovery, police, fires, coastal hazards, and locations listed above. The lab uses an isolated game without changing normal progress. **Erase saved progress** is a separate action from resetting a test run.

Choose **Map layers** to view land, terrain materials, artwork bounds, collisions,
prop shadows and light blockers. Use presets, checkboxes or **Solo**, then click/tap
the world to inspect an entity and its ID. **Free camera** pans by dragging or
WASD/arrows and zooms with the wheel or +/−; **Follow actor** returns to driving.
**Pause simulation** freezes the scene for inspection. **Reset view** or closing
Layers restores normal rendering; **Return to saved game** exits the temporary lab.

Progress and gameplay settings are stored in browser storage when available. If storage is blocked, the game remains playable, but progress may not persist.
