City art combines procedural drawing and self-contained embedded artwork.

`src/assets/characters/` holds the user-supplied PNG portraits for the Open Shore
cast (Mara, Eli, Victor, Celia, Adrian Vale, Leila, Harker, Lena, Iris, Tom, Ruth
and Bea) plus the bundled `officer.png`. They are raster art supplied for Taxi
Chaos and are bundled inline as data URLs with no external image service.

The legacy ids `mia` and `frankie` stay in `src/characters.json` because the
authored legacy mission catalogue and the dialogue preview reference them. Their
supplied rasters are no longer shipped, so each resolves to its own neutral
placeholder card (`legacy-mia.png`, `legacy-frankie.png`): original flat artwork
created for Taxi Chaos with a featureless silhouette and the initial only,
regenerated by `tools/prepare-legacy-portraits.py`. These cards are not a portrait
of any cast member and never stand in for the removed raster art.

Sound FX attribution, source links, licences and editing notes are in
[`audio/CREDITS.md`](audio/CREDITS.md), embedded in the standalone HTML and
accessible from the game's welcome/pause settings.

## Map artwork

`map/shop-tar.png`, `map/shop-rust.png`, `map/shop-concrete.png`, and
`map/tree-worn.png` were generated for Taxi Chaos with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool, using a user-supplied urban scene as a style reference. Prompt
direction was straight orthographic overhead, muted weathered materials, blank
runtime sign panels and no baked cast shadows. The three roofs use patched tar,
rusted corrugated metal and aged concrete; the tree uses an irregular olive/sage
canopy. Transparent export margins were trimmed and sprites resized to 256 px
roofs and a 192 px tree. Exact original prompts remain in Git history.
`map/asphalt.svg` is original vector surface detail.
These assets require no external fonts, images or runtime services.

`map/hospital-structure.png` adapts the user-supplied
`hospital-structure-v2.png` for the two-block Midtown Hospital. OpenAI's built-in
image-generation tool extended the roof vertically and retained its weathered
concrete, skylights, pipes and teal helipad. The original upload remains unchanged.
The adaptation requested a 26:66 footprint by adding matching roof sections,
preserving the circular helipad rather than stretching it, removing the red
cutout fringe and leaving a transparent background without external cast shadows.
Exact original prompts remain in Git history; current crop, metre bounds and
light outline are defined in `src/artwork/catalogue.ts`. The PNG is bundled
inline with a procedural fallback.

`map/luxury-condo.png` is OpenAI built-in image-generation option A, adapted from
Lorenzo's supplied condo to retain its cream roof and pool with minimal visible
facade. It is used by Azure Residences. `map/ocean-crest-condo.png` and
`map/pacific-palm-hotel.png` were generated from scratch with the same built-in tool
for Ocean Crest and Pacific Palm. The prompts request orthographic straight-down
roofs, muted weathered materials, minimal visible walls, transparent backgrounds,
no surrounding ground or cast shadows, and no text. Ocean Crest has a slate roof,
terracotta roof terrace and lap pool; Pacific Palm has a long seafoam/cream roof,
HVAC plant, blue skylights and a rooftop pool. The hotel roof was extended in depth
to match its 3:1 footprint. Authored draw-time crops exclude faint alpha specks.
All three sprites are bundled inline with procedural fallbacks.

`map/crate-shipping.png`, `map/bakery-front.png` and `map/palm-canopy.png` are original
pixel art created for Taxi Chaos (simple generated artwork, no third-party source) for the
shared catalogue's representative crate, bakery building and typed palm records. All are
small sprites bundled inline with the other map artwork; the palm keeps a transparent
background, and transparent export padding is cropped at draw time.
